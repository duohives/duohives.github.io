Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DuoHives and getting started.

How fast can we get started?
DuoHives connects to your email and calendar in minutes. No SSO rollouts, no CRM migrations, and no engineering tickets. You can invite teammates whenever you’re ready.
Do I need to change my CRM or workflows?
Nope. DuoHives runs in the background to capture context and nudge next steps. If you use a CRM, we augment it — not replace it. If you don’t, you still get perfect follow‑ups.
What data does DuoHives access?
We only access the minimum necessary data (like subjects, bodies, recipients, and meeting info) to create reminders and next‑step nudges. You can revoke access at any time.
Is my data secure?
Yes — data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is fully auditable, and you control what’s connected. Contact us for a security brief if you need more detail.
Can I try DuoHives for free?
Yes. Join Waitlist and invite a few teammates to validate value quickly. Paid plans unlock team‑wide automations and admin controls.
Does DuoHives work with my email provider?
We support Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 out of the box. For others, reach out — we’re expanding integrations based on demand.
