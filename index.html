Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DuoHives and getting started.

How fast can we get started?
DuoHives connects to your Gmail in minutes. No IT setup, no onboarding sessions, no training required. If you can use email, you can use DuoHives—even if you're not technical at all.
How does DuoHives connect with Gmail?
DuoHives runs through a secure Google Chrome extension. Once installed and connected to your Gmail, it automatically starts organizing leads, tracking deals, and helping you follow up — all without leaving your inbox.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and DuoHives never shares or sells your information. You stay fully in control of your account and connected data.
Who is this built for?
DuoHives is built for email-driven SMBs, founders, and small teams who are drowning in busywork. If you run an agency, a consultancy, a small SaaS company, or any business where your pipeline lives in your inbox, this is for you.
How is DuoHives different from a traditional CRM?
Traditional CRMs sit outside your workflow—you have to manually log every email, update every deal, and remember to check in. DuoHives lives inside Gmail and does the work automatically, saving you 15+ hours a week.
Why is there a waitlist? How do I join?
We're onboarding users in small batches to ensure everyone gets a great experience as we refine the product. Joining is simple—just enter your email on our homepage. We're currently prioritising sales leaders in the US and UK, and you'll get early access as spots open up. No credit card required.
